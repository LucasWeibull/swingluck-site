SwingLuck

Film your golf swing, and the app finds one thing for you to work on.

How it works

Film

Put your phone on a stand and hit. The app finds every swing in the recording.

Analysis on the phone

The app follows your body through the swing and measures it, right on the phone.

One focus

A feeling to practise, a drill with video and a goal. When 7 of 10 swings reach the goal on two practice days, you move on.

Your videos stay on your phone. Your account stores your analyses, focuses and the movement in your swings, not video, with a provider in the EU (Stockholm).

You sign in with Apple, Google or email. Your name and email address are only used for your account. No ads and no tracking. Read the privacy policy.
